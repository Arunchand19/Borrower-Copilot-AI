# Three run-throughs

The seeded journeys are available on the landing screen under **See it in action**. Values below are generated by the same rule engine in `src/rules.ts`.

## Priya

**Inputs asked:** wedding costs; personal loan; wanted ₹8,00,000; net income ₹1,10,000; salaried; existing EMI ₹14,000; essentials ₹68,000; age 29; score 780; 4 months savings; no co-applicant; no bounce; 1.5% processing fee; 36 months.

**Outputs:**

- Verdict: **This can fit, with a ceiling**.
- Lender may sanction: approximately **₹11,35,000** at up to approximately ₹41,000 EMI.
- Borrower should use: approximately **₹5,15,000**, keeping the new EMI under approximately **₹16,400**.
- Fair interest: **9.3%-18.0%** for the broad product band; the range is intentionally wide on the high side because the product is unsecured. Estimated APR with the fee: approximately **9.4%-18.1%**.
- Stress ceiling: approximately **₹4,00,000**.

**Card says:** ask for 9.3%-18.0% interest, ask for all-in APR, and do not cross the safe EMI ceiling. Priya should negotiate the amount down from ₹8,00,000 rather than accept the lender's maximum.

## Ravi

**Inputs asked:** second stock line and delivery vehicle; business loan; wanted ₹15,00,000; conservative monthly income ₹60,000; self-employed; no existing EMI; essentials ₹32,000; age 42; score unknown; 3+ years steady; 3 months savings; 40% variable; co-applicant yes; property collateral ₹45,00,000; no bounce; 1.25% processing fee; 60 months.

**Outputs:**

- Verdict: **Borrow less than you asked** with the conservative ₹60,000/month input.
- Product route: **Secured business loan / LAP**.
- Fair interest: **10.5%-14.0%**, because collateral is more than 1.2x the requested amount.
- Lender may sanction approximately **₹11,60,000**; borrower should use approximately **₹8,75,000**, keeping EMI under approximately **₹18,800**. Stress amount is approximately **₹6,55,000**.
- Confidence: **Low**, because the score is unknown, even though the collateral and history narrow the product/rate band.

**Card says:** ask the lender to price this as secured productive borrowing, show official APR including the processing fee, and separate the stock-line and vehicle use cases. Do not accept unsecured personal-loan pricing without a reason.

## Anita

**Inputs asked:** electric scooter for more delivery runs; two-wheeler loan; wanted ₹1,50,000; conservative income ₹28,000; informal; existing EMI ₹6,000; essentials ₹18,500; age 35; score unknown; variable income; no savings; 35% variable; no co-applicant; one recent bounce; 2% processing fee; 36 months.

**Outputs:**

- Verdict: **Pause this loan**.
- Reason: a recent bounce is an immediate cash-flow warning, and the requested scooter would sit on top of existing high-cost app debt.
- Fair rate is **13.0%-21.5%** because the score is unknown and income is informal; the card shows the exact range and APR estimate.
- Stress ceiling is lower than the normal safe ceiling; do not add the scooter EMI before stabilising the three app loans.

**Card says:** do not sign a new loan tomorrow. Ask for statements and a payoff/consolidation plan for the app loans, stop new borrowing, and build at least one month of essentials first.

> Note: the live app is authoritative for exact rounded rupee outputs. The engine is deliberately deterministic, and its reasons are shown beside the numbers so a rule can be changed in a follow-up session.

The adaptive follow-ups each have a numeric effect: income history changes lender FOIR, savings changes safe EMI capacity, variable-income share changes lender capacity, co-applicant changes lender capacity, collateral changes product/rate, bounces can trigger pause, processing fee changes APR, age changes lender capacity at age extremes, and an offered quote changes the negotiation card comparison.
